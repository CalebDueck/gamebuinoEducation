# Final Project Menu

Choose one cartridge idea, then customize it.

- Collector game: grab coins, stars, snacks, or keys.
- Avoider game: dodge falling hazards.
- Door/key game: collect a key before entering a door.
- Reaction game: press the shown button quickly.
- Rhythm buttons game: match a short button pattern.
- Virtual pet: feed, clean, or play with a tiny creature.
- Simple maze: reach an exit without touching walls.
