# Debug Mission 1 - Fix the Broken Badge

Open `broken_badge.ino`.

Expected behavior:

- The Gamebuino shows a title screen first.
- After that, the screen shows `Debug Badge` at the top.
- A small square appears near the lower half of the screen.
- Pressing LEFT moves the square left.
- Pressing RIGHT moves the square right.
- The square stays on screen.
- Pressing A shows a popup that says `A!`.

Rules:

- The sketch has multiple broken lines.
- There are no clues telling you which lines are wrong.
- Fix the sketch by repairing the existing code.
- After each fix, compile again and read the next problem.

Checkpoint:

- The sketch compiles.
- The sketch uploads.
- Every expected behavior above works on hardware.
